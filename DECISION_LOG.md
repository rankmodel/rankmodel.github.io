# Decision Log

A transparent record of the calls that shaped ModelRank. This is the project's
"living research brain" between NotebookLM sessions. Every entry is reversible by
the community via Discussions + PR.

## 2026-08-21 — Honest growth marathon (no manipulation)
- **Decision:** All growth work is genuine. No fake engagement, star farming,
  dummy issues, manufactured velocity, or deceptive marketing.
- **Rationale:** Deceptive tactics violate platform ToS and would destroy the
  one asset we have — independence/credibility. Real value compounds; fake
  velocity craters the moment it's noticed.
- **Action:** README number fixed (150 → 954 verified), one-line `curl` badge
  added, hero + feature grid + FAQ added to the UI, roadmap + this log committed.

## Standing decisions (from RTK.md / methodology)
- **5-dimension composite** (Benchmarks 70 / Recency 15 / Community 10 /
  Efficiency 5 / Reproducibility 0 reserved). Weights live in `config/settings.py`
  and are community-votable.
- **No paid placements for the score.** Visibility can be bought *if labeled*;
  the composite score cannot.
- **Independence is the product.** We build no models, sell no weights.
- **Raw data is public.** `full_rankings_*.csv` + `leaderboard.json` are the
  source of truth for every claim on the site and README.
