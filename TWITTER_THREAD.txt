TWEET 4/10:
The ModelRank score isn't just benchmarks.

It's 5 dimensions:
• Benchmarks 70% (MMLU-Pro, GPQA, HLE, GSM8K, HumanEval)
• Recency 15%
• Community 10%
• Efficiency 5%

Weighted because raw benchmark scores alone can be gamed. Context matters.

---

TWEET 5/10:
The Community dimension runs ELO head-to-head.

Real users pick winners on real tasks. Those outcomes feed into an ELO ladder — same math that ranks chess players.

No lab can pay to move this number. It's determined entirely by head-to-head outcomes.

The leaderboard reflects actual usage.
