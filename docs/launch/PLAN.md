# Launch plan

The goal is **star velocity** (new stars per day), not total stars. Concentrate
promotion into a single 48-hour window, then sustain.

## The 48-hour window

**T-1 (prep):**
- Confirm the live site, badges, and README are correct.
- Prepare all posts in `docs/launch/` (done).
- Line up the X thread, HN submission, and Product Hunt draft.

**Hour 0:**
- Post Show HN (see `show-hn.md`).
- Post the X thread (see `twitter-x.md`).
- Submit to Product Hunt at 12:01am PT for the best day.

**Hour 0-6:**
- Post to r/LocalLLaMA, then r/MachineLearning (see `reddit.md`).
- Publish the Dev.to article (see `devto.md`).
- Reply to every comment within the first few hours (velocity rewards engagement).

**Hour 6-48:**
- Cross-post to r/selfhosted and r/artificial.
- Share the weekly newsletter signup.
- Thank stargazers publicly; ask which model to score next.

## The 7-day sustain

- Day 3: A "how the scoring works" mini-thread (methodology highlight).
- Day 5: Spotlight one community-contributed model or badge.
- Day 7: Recap of top movers from the week.
- Ongoing: respond to issues/PRs fast, label good-first-issues, keep the weekly
  newsletter shipping.

## Metrics to watch
- Stars/day (the trending signal).
- Referrals from HN/Reddit/Product Hunt.
- Badge embeds created (the real growth loop).
