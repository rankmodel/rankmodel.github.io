Show HN: ModelRank — an independent, open leaderboard for AI models (no paid placements, weights are public)

---

Founder here. Every other leaderboard we looked at either has a conflict of interest or hides its scoring. ModelRank doesn't: the 5-dimension weights live in config/settings.py and the community votes on them in Discussions.

To keep us honest, we publish the raw data — there's a CSV/JSON of every model's benchmark, efficiency, community, recency, and reproducibility score in the repo and on the site. Scrutinize it.

The most surprising real result in our index: the efficiency leader is a 135M-parameter model that outscores the top composite model on efficiency by a wide margin. Small models are wildly underrated on value. AMA.
