Frequently Asked Questions

How is the score calculated?

We use a composite scoring algorithm that evaluates 5 dimensions: independent benchmarks, computational efficiency, community engagement, model architecture, and freshness. All scores are objective and verified.

Do I need an account?

No! The free tier works instantly for any publicly available HuggingFace model. Just copy the badge URL. For Pro and Featured tiers, we'll send you a secure management link.

Can I embed badges in my README?

Yes. All badges are served as highly-optimized SVGs that work perfectly in HuggingFace model cards, GitHub READMEs, and personal websites.

What is the refund policy?

We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee for Pro subscriptions if you're not satisfied with the premium badges or priority scoring.
